# WebKit virtualization: missing rows or missing paint?

Checked 2026-11-23. Native scrolling can expose an area before the application mounts its rows, or before WebKit paints them. In [Garcon's 98-entry GTK 2.50.6 experiment](scroll-row-stripe.md), all three production down trains exposed the sizer across the full height of **one column**. On the diagnostic build, a band up to 120 px appeared in 22/67 reads during one down train even with all 100 wrappers retained (98 messages). More DOM overscan is not a sufficient explanation. Xvfb samples are not physical frames; late rAF callbacks do not establish missing pixels.

A [two-column follow-up](scroll-row-paired-stripes.md) found matching sizer bands 1,004 px apart in three of four normal trains with zero viewport scroll-offset writes. No bands appeared in two absolute-`top` trains or in the late window. The custom scrollbar was still hover-visible, so this was not a reproduction of the upstream fade.

**Two discriminators, not production changes**

1. Keep all rows mounted; change only [absolute wrapper positioning](../../../web/src/lib/components/chat/transcript/ConversationFeedVirtualRow.svelte#L94-L103) from `translateY(start)` to `top: start`. Match rectangles, renderer, anchor and input. Keep muted-row controls; sample two separated columns. Separately timestamp [Virt scroll-offset writes](../../../web/src/lib/virt/virtual-list-dom-driver.ts#L91-L98) without adding layout reads. A band with no Virt writes rules out an overlapping Virt correction for that train, not all compositor causes.
2. Compare already-available GTK 2.50.6 and newer builds containing the tile fix. Match app, input, display backend, actual renderer, scale and viewport. Separate during-wheel samples from post-settle scrollbar fade. A version difference does not isolate one patch.

For both: keep keyed rows and native scrolling; require unchanged selection endpoints, focused control and hit target, and the same reading row within 1 CSS px across a prepend. Check sequential screen-reader navigation on the actual target; do not ship placeholder-only content or disable momentum to pass a pixel test.

**GTK availability (amd64, 2026-11-23).** The Bookworm runner has GTK3/libsoup3 WebKit, JavaScriptCore and driver `2.50.6-1~deb12u2`; its release, security and backports indexes offer no newer qualifying build. The feature gate matters: upstream 2.50.6 defaults [damage propagation on for GTK](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/webkitgtk-2.50.6/Source/WTF/Scripts/Preferences/UnifiedWebPreferences.yaml#L6019-L6033) but [use of damage for compositing off](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/webkitgtk-2.50.6/Source/WTF/Scripts/Preferences/UnifiedWebPreferences.yaml#L7980-L7993). The [compositor](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/webkitgtk-2.50.6/Source/WebKit/WebProcess/WebPage/CoordinatedGraphics/ThreadedCompositor.cpp#L270-L299) passes collected damage to the surface separately from clipping its own painting. Neither the observed stripe nor compilation of that code proves the damaged-region redraw path was active.

No second matched runtime was installed. Noble offers `2.52.6-0ubuntu0.24.04.2` (dry run on this host: 47.4 MB download, 176 MB installed), but its Mesa 25.2.8, GTK 3.24.41 and Xvfb 21.1.12 differ from Bookworm's 22.3.6, 3.24.38 and 21.1.7. For a future A/B, use two copies of **one** Trixie rootfs: the [2.50.6-1~deb13u1 snapshot](https://snapshot.debian.org/mr/package/webkit2gtk/2.50.6-1~deb13u1/binpackages) and [2.52.6-1~deb13u1 archive](https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/w/webkit2gtk/) publish matched `libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0`, `libjavascriptcoregtk-4.1-0` and `webkitgtk-webdriver`. The [2.52.6 tag contains the damage addition](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/webkitgtk-2.52.6/Source/WebCore/platform/graphics/texmap/coordinated/CoordinatedBackingStoreProxy.cpp#L332-L342); neither inspected Trixie nor Noble 2.52.6 packaging patch modifies that site. Pin exact versions (security may be newer), reject unrelated dependency changes, and record actual renderer/driver and shared-memory vs DMA-BUF before comparing. No cross-suite installation or browser execution was used to establish availability.

**Search limits:** Searched WebKit Bugzilla and PRs (virtualized, scrolling, tiles, transforms) and Virtua, react-window and TanStack issues (blanking, overscan). No verified match to our retained-row GTK case. Upstream reproductions were read, not run.
