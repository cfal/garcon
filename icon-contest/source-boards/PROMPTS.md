## Google board 01

- Model: `gemini-3-pro-image`
- Aspect ratio: `1:1`
- Image size: `2K`

> Create a precise 4 by 4 design exploration board containing sixteen distinct minimal app icon concepts for Garcon, a self-hosted visual workspace for coding agents. The brand personality is capable, composed, technical, friendly, and independent. Explore these motifs without copying existing logos: a French garçon or bellhop cap reduced to two or three geometric strokes; a cap whose brim becomes a speech-bubble tail; a speech bubble containing a G made from negative space; four tiled workspace panes inside a bubble; a small constellation of agent nodes inside a chat shape; a monoline G that also reads as a terminal prompt or window. Each cell must contain exactly one centered symbol with consistent optical size and generous padding. Flat vector-like geometry, strong silhouette, one or two colors only, royal blue with white or charcoal. Must remain recognizable at 16 pixels and be easy to reconstruct as SVG. Use a clean neutral background and an exact equal grid. No words, no labels, no letters except deliberate G monograms, no numbers, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockups, no mascots, no faces, no ornate heraldry, no watermark, no existing brand marks.
